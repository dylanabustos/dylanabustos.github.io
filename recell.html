Understanding the Problem

ReCell is a startup in the used device market. They needed a way to dynamically price used phones and tablets based on device specifications rather than relying on fixed depreciation tables. The goal was to build a model that predicts the normalized resale price of a device.

  • 3,454 used devices in the dataset
  • Features: brand, OS, screen size, camera, RAM, battery, days used
  • Target variable: normalized_used_price
  • 93% of devices in the dataset run Android

Exploring the Data

Before modeling, the data was explored to understand how devices were distributed across brands and release years. The dataset spanned devices from 2013 to 2020, with 2014 being the most represented year.

  • 2014 had the most devices (642), suggesting a large secondhand market for that era
  • Samsung was the most common named brand with 341 devices
  • Huawei (251), LG (201), and Lenovo (171) rounded out the top brands
  • Newer devices (2019–2020) had fewer entries — less time to enter the used market

What Hardware Features Drive Price?

Correlation analysis was used to identify which features had the strongest relationship with resale price. Screen size, battery, and camera quality were the top predictors.

  • Screen size had the strongest correlation with used price (0.62)
  • Battery capacity was nearly as important (0.61)
  • Main camera megapixels had a strong correlation (0.59)
  • Days used had a negative correlation (-0.36) — older devices are worth less

Does 4G or 5G Connectivity Matter?

Network connectivity turned out to be a meaningful price differentiator. Devices with 5G commanded a significant premium over non-5G devices in the used market.

  • 5G devices had an avg used price of 5.23 vs 4.33 for non-5G — a 21% premium
  • 4G devices were priced 18% higher than non-4G devices on average
  • Only 152 of 3,454 devices (4.4%) had 5G — still rare in the dataset
  • Connectivity is a strong signal worth including in the pricing model

Avg Used Price by OS

iOS devices (Apple) commanded the highest average resale price, consistent with Apple's reputation for strong device longevity and brand value retention in the used market.

  • iOS devices had the highest avg used price at 4.96
  • Android devices averaged 4.41
  • Windows devices averaged 4.16
  • Apple's strong brand value is clearly reflected in secondhand pricing

Building the Model

A linear regression model was built using the most predictive features identified in the exploratory phase. The model was evaluated on a held-out test set using R² and RMSE.

  • Features used: screen size, battery, camera, RAM, days used, 4G/5G, OS
  • Data split 70/30 into training and test sets
  • Linear regression chosen for interpretability of coefficients
  • Model performance evaluated using R² score and RMSE on test data
7

Conclusion & Recommendations

The model successfully learned the relationship between device specs and resale value. The findings give ReCell a data-driven foundation for dynamic pricing.

  • Prioritize screen size, battery, and camera as primary pricing signals
  • Apply a 5G premium — those devices command ~21% more in the used market
  • iOS devices should be listed at a premium over comparable Android devices
  • Days used should be factored into price — each additional day reduces value
