New Wheels – SQL Analytics

Built a SQL dashboard and automated quarterly reports to identify key business drivers and customer retention strategies using analysis of 994 annual customer reviews. Answered 10 business questions covering customer distribution, revenue trends, vehicle preferences, and shipping performance.

TypeSQL Analytics
ToolsSQL, MySQL
Dataset994 Customer Reviews
SQLMySQLAnalyticsBusiness InsightsReporting
Business Questions & Insights
Question 1
Find the total number of customers who have placed orders. What is the distribution of customers across states?
Insights
  • 994 total customers across all states
  • Texas and California had the most customers (97), followed by Florida (86) and New York (69)
  • Popular states held the most customers overall
Query & Output
Question 2
Which are the top 5 vehicle makers preferred by customers?
Insights
  • Chevrolet, Ford, Toyota, Pontiac, and Dodge were the top 5 preferred makers
  • Popularity likely driven by affordability and reliability of these brands
Query & Output
Question 3
Which is the most preferred vehicle maker in each state?
Insights
  • Chevrolet was the most preferred vehicle maker in the majority of states
Question 4
Find the overall average rating given by customers. What is the average rating in each quarter?
Insights
  • Overall average customer rating was 3.14 out of 5
  • Average rating trended downward as each quarter progressed
Query
Question 5
Find the percentage distribution of feedback from customers. Are customers getting more dissatisfied over time?
Insights
  • Yes — by Q4, "very bad" feedback rose to 30.65% from just 10.97% in Q1
  • Customer satisfaction declined significantly across the year
Query & Output
Question 6
What is the trend of the number of orders by quarter?
Insights
  • Total orders dropped by Q4
  • Decline likely due to lack of inventory or delayed order fulfillment
Query & Output
Question 7
Calculate the net revenue generated by the company. What is the quarter-over-quarter % change in net revenue?
Insights
  • Net revenue declined each quarter, resulting in negative quarter-over-quarter % changes
  • Q2 and Q3 showed the largest quarter-over-quarter percentage drops
Query
Question 8
What is the trend of net revenue and orders by quarter?
Insights
  • Total orders declined each quarter, directly driving the decline in net revenue
Question 9
What is the average discount offered for different types of credit cards?
Insights
  • Credit card discounts ranged from 58% to 64% across card types
  • Some customers may prioritize other card benefits over higher discount rates
Question 10
What is the average time taken to ship placed orders for each quarter?
Insights
  • Q4 had the highest average shipping time, likely due to holiday demand
  • Increased shipping days may be caused by inventory shortages or weather delays
Business Recommendations
Account for holidays and bad weather seasons when planning inventory and shipping capacity.
Promote credit card discounts or partner with a credit card company to offer sign-up incentives.
Maintain adequate inventory of the most popular vehicle makes, especially Chevrolet and Ford.
Define a clear inventory focus — luxury vs. reliable vehicles — to better target customer segments.
Increase marketing efforts in lower-performing states to grow the customer base.
Investigate the root causes of declining Q4 ratings and address fixable issues proactively.
Prioritize stocking vehicles preferred by customers in the highest-ordering states.
